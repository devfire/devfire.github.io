01 The gap

Most EdTech AI fails on trust, not capability.

A tutor that confidently hallucinates in front of a thirteen-year-old. A copilot that ignores the curriculum it's meant to support. A dashboard no teacher asked for, grading them on metrics they never chose. The model is rarely the problem.

The gap between what a model can do and what a classroom will accept is. That gap is where we work.

02 What we do

Four ways we help.

  1. 01

    Strategy & roadmapping

    Where AI creates real value in your product — and where it's a liability. You leave with a prioritized, evidence-based roadmap your team and your board can defend.

  2. 02

    Responsible AI & evaluation

    Evals, benchmarks, and guardrails for accuracy, bias, safety, and FERPA- and COPPA-aligned data practice. So you can ship with confidence — and, when asked, defend it.

  3. 03

    Design & prototyping

    Pedagogically grounded UX and working prototypes your team can put in front of real educators in weeks, not quarters.

  4. 04

    Implementation & enablement

    Senior engineers embedded with yours to ship production AI — retrieval over your own content, tutoring agents, feedback tools — and leave your team able to maintain it.

03 How we work

Fixed scope. Senior people. No body-shopping.

We work in clear phases with a small team. The people who scope the work are the people who do the work.

Diagnose 2–3 weeks

We embed with your team, audit your data and product, and find where AI earns its keep — and where it doesn't. You get a sharp, honest recommendation, not a deck.

Build 6–12 weeks

We design, prototype, and ship alongside your engineers. Evals first, features second. Every release gets more defensible than the last.

Hand off Ongoing

Documentation, runbooks, and a team that owns what we built. We stay on call, but we're never the dependency.

04 What we believe

Four convictions that shape every engagement.

Accuracy is a feature, not a stretch goal.

A confident wrong answer is worse than no answer — especially for a learner who trusts it.

Teachers are the decision-makers.

Build for the person who decides whether your product stays in the room.

Reduce load. Don't add surveillance.

The best AI gives teachers time back. It doesn't grade them on it.

Ship the smallest thing that helps a learner this week.

Momentum beats monuments. Real users beat demos.

06 About

Between two worlds that rarely meet.

Devfire was founded on a simple frustration: too much AI in education is built by people who've never taught, and too much education software is built by people who've never shipped AI. We sit between those worlds — senior practitioners in machine learning, product, and learning science who've shipped production systems and spent real time in classrooms.

We stay small on purpose. It keeps the work senior, the communication direct, and the accountability absolute. When you hire Devfire, you get Devfire.
